The benchmark uses scikit-learn's packaged copy. It derives a fixed held-out subset,
probabilities from a model fitted on separate training rows, deliberately distorted
probabilities, and row-derived identifiers. These are alterations; no creator endorsement
is implied. Generated row-level data are not committed or included in the compact bundle.

The repeated-patient fixture is wholly synthetic and generated by the repository's own
benchmark code. It is not clinical validation and contains no real patient records.

The benchmark's original code is covered by the repository's Apache-2.0 LICENSE.
No source-only competitor package is redistributed in the compact review bundle.
